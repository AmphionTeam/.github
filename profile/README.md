# Amphion Team

We build open-source models, datasets and benchmarks for speech, audio and music: neural codecs, spoken language models, voice agents, and the tools to evaluate them.

<!-- TODO: add one sentence on who you are, e.g. affiliation and how this org relates to the Amphion toolkit. -->

This organization hosts the research projects of the team behind the [Amphion](https://github.com/open-mmlab/Amphion) toolkit. Most projects come with a paper, released checkpoints or data, and a demo page.

[Amphion toolkit](https://github.com/open-mmlab/Amphion) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/amphion) · [X / Twitter](https://x.com/realamphion)

## Spoken language models and voice agents

|---|---|---|---|

| [FlexiSLM](https://github.com/AmphionTeam/FlexiSLM) | A spoken language model with dynamic and controllable frame rates | EMNLP 2026 | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31247) · [Demo](https://flexislm.github.io) |

## Datasets and benchmarks

| Project | What it does | Venue | Links |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Emilia-NV](https://github.com/AmphionTeam/Emilia-NV) | Mandarin speech dataset with word-level annotation of non-verbal vocalizations | arXiv 2025 | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04195) · [Demo](https://nvspeech170k.github.io/) · [Data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/amphion/Emilia-NV) |
| [NV-Bench](https://github.com/AmphionTeam/NV-Bench) | Benchmark for non-verbal vocalization synthesis in expressive text-to-speech | arXiv 2026 | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15352) · [Demo](https://charlesnii.github.io/nvbench.github.io/) |
| [SpeechJudge](https://github.com/AmphionTeam/SpeechJudge) | Dataset, benchmark and reward model for judging speech naturalness | arXiv 2025 | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07931) · [Demo](https://speechjudge.github.io/) |
| [VoxSafeBench](https://github.com/AmphionTeam/VoxSafeBench) | Safety, fairness and privacy benchmark for speech language models | arXiv 2026 | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14548) · [Demo](https://amphionteam.github.io/VoxSafeBench_demopage/) |
| [SD-Eval](https://github.com/AmphionTeam/SD-Eval) | Benchmark for spoken dialogue understanding beyond words | NeurIPS 2024 | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13340) · [Data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/amphion/SD-Eval) |
| [SA-Eval](https://github.com/AmphionTeam/SA-Eval) | Benchmark for models that take spoken instructions mixed with audio | arXiv 2025 | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15338) |
| [RealComm](https://github.com/AmphionTeam/RealComm) | Audio deepfake detection over real communication channels | | [Project page](https://wwwwwli.github.io/RealComm/) |
